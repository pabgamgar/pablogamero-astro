Submoney is a minimal subscription and bill manager focused on a single question: how much money should be in your bank account today to cover every upcoming charge. It keeps the interface light and practical:

- Daily balance you need to cover every subscription, recurring payment and bill
- Money set aside per card or account, going down as each payment goes out
- Calendar of upcoming charges, with reminders before each one and before trials convert
- A review every few months, with a Cancel list for the ones you drop
- Pause until a date, split costs, other currencies and price history
- Home screen widgets, available in 20 languages

No sign-up and no bank connection: your data stays on your phone. It is free for up to 15 items, with a one-time Plus purchase for the rest.

The goal is to keep your subscription buffer clear and avoid surprise overdrafts.
